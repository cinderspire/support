MoveCalc

A moving planner: price the move, count what you own, label and scan boxes, follow an 8-week checklist and track the budget. iPhone app.

Questions

Does MoveCalc need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no cloud. Your data stays on your phone.

Are the costs quotes?

No. They are planning estimates with a range, built from published rate tables reviewed in November 2026. Always get written quotes and check a company's licence and insurance.

How do I scan a box label?

Open Boxes, tap the scanner icon, tap Start camera and point it at the QR label. You can also type the six-character code printed under the QR.

Can I print the labels?

Yes. In Boxes use the label menu to share the PDF or print all labels, or open one box and tap Share or Print. Sheets use US letter or A4 depending on your country setting.

Do the reminders work offline?

Yes. They are local notifications on your phone. Switch them on in the Plan tab.

How do I start again for a new move?

Settings, then Start a new move. It erases your boxes, photos, inventory, budget and checklist after you confirm.

Which countries are supported?

United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia, with the right currency, units and wording for each.

Paycheck Planner

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Paycheck Planner ("the app") is published by Cinderspire. Cinderspire is the iOS publisher identity. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your numbers stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

Salaries, hourly rates, shifts, saved scenarios, history and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. We do not have an account system and we cannot see this information. Erase it any time in Settings, Erase my data, or by uninstalling the app.

What the app sends

The app can ask a promotion service for a small "from the makers of this app" card. That request contains the app identifier, the operating system (iOS or Android), the device class (phone or tablet) and a promotion group name. It contains no name, email, advertising identifier, location or any number you typed. If you tap a card, the link opens in your browser and our server records that the card was opened. If the request fails, the app shows a built-in link to salarycalculator.us instead. Network connections use HTTPS.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • No sale of personal information.

Sharing and exports

When you share a result image, PDF or CSV, the file is created on your phone and handed to the share sheet you choose. We do not receive it.

External links

The app links to irs.gov, ssa.gov, state revenue department websites and salarycalculator.us. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is meant for working adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not tax advice; not a government app

Paycheck Planner provides estimates for planning. It is not tax advice and is not affiliated with the IRS, the Social Security Administration or any government agency.

Changes and contact

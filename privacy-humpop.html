Privacy Policy

H

Hum & Pop: ASMR Toys

A box of touch-and-sound toys: singing glasses, bubble wrap, slime, sand and soap. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

Hum & Pop collects no data. Your play days, stats and settings are saved on your device, and nothing about how you play is sent anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking. The app never uses the microphone or the camera.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing about how you play. Play days, streaks, play time, favourite and recent toys, saved mixes, your own haptic patterns and your settings are stored on your device. Every sound is inside the app, so nothing is downloaded while you play. There is no developer-run server and no cloud save.
  • Whether you own Hum & Pop Pass. Apple's App Store tells the app whether you own the Hum & Pop Pass purchase, and the app keeps that answer on your device.
  • Only what you choose to email us. If you write to support@playtools.top, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • location
  • photos
  • sound recordings — the app never uses the microphone
  • browsing history
  • payment card details
  • usage analytics — the app includes no analytics SDK
  • crash reports — the app includes no crash-reporting SDK
  • identifiers that could track you across other companies' apps or websites — the app does no tracking

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything the app saves is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

The Hum & Pop Pass purchase is processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details, and the app keeps only one thing from the App Store: whether you own Hum & Pop Pass.

Third-party services

Apple's App Store is the only outside service the app talks to. It has its own privacy policy.

  • Apple's App Store — Shows the Hum & Pop Pass price, processes the purchase, restores it, and tells the app whether you own it. It also shows the standard rating prompt, which the app may ask for after a finished bubble sheet or a new play day. Apple privacy policy.

The Support, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links in the app open web pages only when you tap them. Like any website, the site that serves a page receives the ordinary information a browser sends to load it.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • In Settings, Reset play days & stats deletes your play days, streaks and play statistics from the device. It cannot be undone. Your Hum & Pop Pass purchase is kept, because it belongs to your Apple Account.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Hum & Pop is made for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 — there is no account or sign-up, and nothing about the player is sent to us. If you have a question about a child's use of the app, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a change in the services it uses — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

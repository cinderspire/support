Privacy Policy

Wedding Invites Studio · updated November 9, 2026

In short: your designs, photo and guest list stay on your device. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising identifier.

What stays on your device

Your couple details, saved designs, the one photo you choose, your guest list (names, groups, replies, meal choices, optional phone numbers and emails and notes) and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. Cinderspire has no server that receives them and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your photo

The app uses the system photo picker. It does not ask for access to your photo library and only receives the single photo you select. The copy is kept inside the app and removed when you delete the design or the app.

What you export or send

PNG, PDF and CSV files are created on your device and handed to the app you choose in the share sheet (Files, Mail, Messages, a printer app). Reminder messages open your own Messages or Mail app, filled in; nothing is sent until you send it. Once a file leaves the app, the privacy practices of the receiving app apply.

The one network request

The app can ask a promotion service for a small card shown at the bottom of some screens (for example a link to weddinginvites.us). The request contains the app identifier, the platform, a content category and whether the device is a phone or tablet. It contains no name, email, advertising identifier, guest data or design data. Tapping a card opens your browser or store. If the request fails, the app simply shows a built-in suggestion or nothing.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is made for adults planning a wedding and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app collects no personal data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears above and the app's store listing is updated.

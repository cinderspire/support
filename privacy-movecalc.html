MoveCalc privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Published by Cinderspire.

Short version: MoveCalc has no account and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your boxes, their contents, optional box photos, inventory, budget, checklist progress, country, units and moving day are stored only on your device. We cannot see them and they are not uploaded. Deleting the app, or using "Start a new move" in Settings, erases them.

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR labels on your boxes and, if you choose to take one, a photo of a box. Camera access is requested only when you tap Start camera or Take photo. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Photos are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Notifications

If you switch on plan reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. No server is involved. You can switch them off at any time.

Network requests

The app can load a small promotional card about our other products. The request contains the app name, the operating system type, the device class (phone or tablet) and a section name. It contains no advertising identifier, account, location, or personal data. We may count that the card was shown or tapped. Links in the app open in your browser, which is governed by that site's own policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact lists.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No sale of data.

Children

MoveCalc is intended for adults arranging a household move and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Estimates

Cost figures are planning estimates, not quotes, and MoveCalc is not affiliated with any government body or moving company.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date. Questions: support@playtools.top.

Questions

Does the app show live prices?
No. You type every price yourself. There is no market data and no broker connection.

Where is my journal stored?
Only on your device. Settings has CSV export and Erase all data.

How is annualised return calculated?
By default as the trade's return x 365 / days to expiry (simple). Settings lets you choose compounded and/or a 252-trading-day year. The convention is printed under each result.

Which model prices the Greeks?
Black-Scholes-Merton with a continuous dividend yield, time = days / 365. It prices European options; US options can be exercised early, so real prices can differ. Theta is per calendar day, vega per 1 volatility point, rho per 1 percentage point of the rate.

How is the wheel cost basis calculated?
Adjusted basis = (cost of the shares you hold minus the net premium kept since you last sold out) / shares. Buy-backs and fees reduce the premium.

Why did the implied volatility solver say there is no solution?
The premium is below the lowest or above the highest value the model allows for those inputs. Check that the premium is per share and that stock price, strike, days and rate are correct.

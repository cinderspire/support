Privacy

Contractor Pay UK privacy policy

Published by Cinderspire. Last updated 8 November 2026.

The short version

Your day rate, costs, saved scenarios and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no account. We do not use analytics or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell data.

What is stored on your device

The numbers you type, the scenarios you save, your theme and reminder choices, and which dates you asked to be reminded about. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app sends

The app may ask our server for a small promotional card (for example, a link to our calculator websites). The request contains the app name, the platform (iOS or Android) and a content group. It contains no identifier, no location and none of your numbers. If we cannot be reached, the app simply shows a built-in card or nothing.

Links to other sites

Some buttons open pages in your browser (gov.uk guidance, our calculator website). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Notifications, only when you switch on a reminder. Internet access, for the promotional card and links. Nothing else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Estimates only

Results are estimates, not tax, legal or financial advice. The app is not affiliated with any government body.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@playtools.top.

Cost of Living UK

What your money was worth, your own inflation rate, and how UK places compare. From Cinderspire.

Slide two markers along a price timeline from January 1988, weight 17 spending categories to find your own rate, compare rents, house prices and council tax by area, and work out the pay that keeps your housing budget level when you move. It works offline and shows when its data was updated.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

Official open data: consumer price indices and private rents from the Office for National Statistics, the UK House Price Index from HM Land Registry, and council tax statistics for England from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government. All licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The app is not affiliated with any government body.

How current is it?

Inflation figures are published monthly. The app shows "Data updated" with the release date, and looks for newer data in the background at most every six hours.

Why is a place missing a figure?

We only show figures that an official source publishes. Rents cover England, Wales and Scotland; council tax is for England; Northern Ireland has house prices only. We never invent a cost index for a city.

Is this financial advice?

No. Results are estimates for planning. Check your own circumstances before making decisions.

Does it need an account or collect my data?

No account. Your inputs stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

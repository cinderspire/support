Post Preview Studio

Frame, split and plan photos. Support and FAQ.

What it does

Post Preview Studio helps you get images ready before you post: frame a photo at common ratios with neutral guides, split a wide photo into seamless swipe tiles, arrange a three-column grid plan and write captions with a live counter.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos, plans and drafts stay on your device.

Does the export have a watermark or location data?

No watermark and no location data. Exports are cut from your original file at full resolution.

Why does the app ask to add photos?

Only to save your exported crops and tiles when you tap Save to Photos. The app uses the system photo picker and cannot browse your library.

Which languages are supported?

The app interface is in English. The store listing is available in several languages.

Is it connected to a social network?

No. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any social network or platform. The guides are generic.

How do I reorder my plan?

In Plan, press and hold a photo and drag it to a new place, or tap it and use Earlier or Later.

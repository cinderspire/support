TaskFlow: To-Do & Planner

To-do list, calendar, focus timer and statistics. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Your tasks, calendar entries, focus sessions and statistics never leave your device. From version 1.1.15, TaskFlow sends technical error reports and four short purchase-flow records to Sentry, the error-reporting service we use; they carry no identifier and none of your entries (see “Error reports” below). The app has no advertising SDK and shows no third-party ads. The free version shows a small card for three of our other apps; from version 1.1.15 it comes only from a list inside the app, which downloads nothing and sends nothing (see “Cards for our own apps” below).

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing you type. Tasks, notes, calendar entries, focus sessions and statistics are stored on your device, inside the app. There is no developer-run server and no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Whether Premium is active. Apple tells the app whether your entitlement is active. Purchases go directly through Apple's StoreKit, with no third-party purchase service in between.
  • Error reports (from version 1.1.15). When the app crashes or freezes, or when an App Store purchase, a restore or loading the prices fails, the app sends a technical error report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., EU data region). It also sends four short purchase-flow records: plans loaded (sent when the app starts), Premium screen shown, purchase started, and its result with the plan's product id. A report contains what went wrong and where in the app's code, the app version, technical details of the device (model, iOS version, memory, screen size, language and time zone) and, for a purchase problem, the plan's product id and the App Store's error code. It contains no name, email address, account or device identifier and no screenshot, the app does not add your tasks, notes, habits or other entries to it, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Reports are deleted automatically within Sentry's retention period (at most 90 days). The network connection reveals an IP address to Sentry; the app does not ask Sentry to store it.
  • Cards for our own apps. The free version shows a card for one of three of our other apps at the end of the Tasks list and on the Stats screen, and Settings → More from us lists them. The cards come from a list inside the app, and nothing is sent for them. Premium removes the cards. See “Cards for our own apps” below.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your advertising identifier (IDFA) or any tracking data — the app does not track you and does not ask for tracking permission
  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • precise location
  • photos or files outside the app
  • browsing history
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details — the store only tells the app whether your entitlement is active.

Third-party services

Apart from Apple's App Store for purchases and Sentry for the error reports described above, the app uses no outside service from version 1.1.15 (versions 1.1.13 and 1.1.14 also read a settings file from GitHub Pages, see “Cards for our own apps”). The app links no third-party advertising SDK.

Cards for our own apps

The card is our own cross-promotion for three other apps of ours on the App Store: Budget Planner, MindFlow and Gabby. From version 1.1.15 it comes only from a list that ships inside the app: the app downloads nothing for it and sends nothing about it, no card links to a website, and tapping a card’s button opens that app’s page in the App Store. Cards never appear in editors, on the Premium screen or during a Focus session, and Premium removes them.

Versions 1.1.13 and 1.1.14 also download one small public settings file from cinderspire.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages) once per app start, when a card is due; like any website, GitHub sees your IP address for that request. That file could switch on cards from our promo service, but it has always been switched off, so those versions too show only the cards from their built-in list, which also names one of our websites. Version 1.1.15 no longer downloads the file.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stored on your device.
  • Where the app offers a reset or delete option in its settings, using it clears the data you chose to remove.
  • You can delete individual tasks and lists inside the app at any time.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

TaskFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

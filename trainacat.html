TrainACat

A calm, kind cat-training coach for your pocket.

A built-in clicker with three sounds and haptics, 34 short lessons on a friendly path, a profile and progress for each cat, an age-based weekly plan with gentle reminders, a body-language reader and a problem solver. It works offline and needs no account.

Questions

Does it need an account?
No. Everything works offline and your cats' data stays on your device.
Is it veterinary advice?
No. It shares general reward-based training ideas. If your cat seems unwell or behaves differently all of a sudden, please ask your vet.
How do I turn on reminders?
Plan tab, switch on Gentle reminders, choose days and a time, allow notifications when asked.
How do I delete my data?
Settings, Erase all my data, or delete the app.

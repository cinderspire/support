Privacy Policy

W

Work Hours & Days UK

Hours worked, working days between dates, annual leave, UK bank holidays and week numbers. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version

Everything you enter — shift times, dates, leave settings, your region and saved results — stays on your iPhone. The iPhone app collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content. Calculations and the bundled bank holiday dates work offline.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything the app keeps or hands on.

  • What you type stays on the device. Shift times, breaks, dates, leave settings, your default region, theme and up to 5 saved results (unlimited with Pro) are stored inside the app on your iPhone. There is no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time Pro purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • CSV export (Pro). When you export your saved results, a CSV file is handed to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Links you tap. “Privacy policy” in Settings opens this page in your browser; “Contact” opens your email app.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the iPhone app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • contacts, photos or files
  • location
  • your working hours, dates, leave or saved results
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details — Apple only tells the app whether Pro is unlocked.

Third-party services

The iPhone app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

  • Apple App Store — handles the optional Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. Apple privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • You can delete any saved result in History (swipe it away) or clear the whole history at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Work Hours & Days UK is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

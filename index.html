How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID (iOS) or same Google account (Android) that made the purchase, open the app, go to its Premium or Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. Then close the app fully and reopen it.

Still missing? Email us and say which app and store, and roughly when you bought it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app — so you cancel them in the store, and the cancellation takes effect immediately on your side.

On iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select the subscription, then tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also go straight there in a browser: Manage your Apple subscriptions.

On Android:

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile picture, top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Select the subscription, then tap Cancel subscription.

Two things worth knowing. Cancelling stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. And deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: you have to cancel it in the store, or it keeps renewing.

How do free trials work?

Where an app offers a free trial, you get full access for the trial period and are only charged when the trial ends. To avoid the charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends — cancelling still leaves you the rest of the trial.

Each app's page lists exactly which of its plans include a trial.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, not by us. We never see your card details and we cannot issue or reverse a payment — only Apple or Google can do that.

  • Apple (App Store): go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.
  • Google (Play Store): open the Play Store, go to your order history under Payments & subscriptions, find the purchase and request a refund there.

If you are asking for a refund because something is broken, please email us as well. We would much rather fix the problem — and if we can, you may not need the refund at all.

Where is my data stored?

Our apps store what you enter on your device. There is no account to create, and we do not collect personal data about you or sell data.

Because the data lives on the device, deleting an app deletes its data too. Apps that are free and ad-supported show ads served by third-party ad networks, which process limited device information to deliver those ads; this is described in each app’s own privacy policy.

An app crashes or misbehaves — what should I do?

Update to the latest version, fully close the app and reopen it, and restart your device if that did not help.

If it persists, email us with your device model, your iOS or Android version, the app name and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps.

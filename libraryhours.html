Library Hours Now

Which library is open right now, and until when.

Search a city or use your location to see libraries with the open ones first: open until 8:00 PM, closing in 45 minutes, or opens tomorrow at 9:00 AM, always in the library's own time zone. Save favourites, get a reminder before closing, and study in focused blocks with a simple timer.

Questions

Where do the opening hours come from?

Mostly from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; community data, about 25,000 libraries) and partly from libraryhours24.com. Each library shows its source and the date of the list.

The hours are wrong. What can I do?

Open the library and tap Report wrong hours. It opens an email with the library details filled in. Please also tell the library.

Does it need my location?

No. Search a city, or tap Use my location once to sort by distance. The position stays on your device.

Why does a library say Closed today on a holiday?

Public-holiday closures are typical patterns, not confirmed by each library. Check with the library.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app includes a list of libraries and refreshes it when you are online.

How do reminders work?

Save a library, open it and switch on Reminder. You get a local notification shortly before closing. Change the lead time in More.

Privacy Policy

L

LiveSight - AI Navigation

Live camera and voice assistant for blind and low-vision people. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

LiveSight has to see and hear to help you. Only while a session is running, camera frames, microphone audio and your location go directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key, and Google sends back speech and text. To show the weather, your coordinates go to Open-Meteo once per launch. LiveSight sends nothing to us — we never receive any of it.

There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. We never ask for your name or email address to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere. None of it is sent to us.

  • What the camera sees and what you say, while a session is running. Only while a LiveSight session is running, the app sends the following to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC, generativelanguage.googleapis.com), using your own API key: camera frames (1 to 4 per second depending on the mode, or the rate you choose in Settings, up to 10; downscaled to 512×288 JPEG images), microphone audio (16 kHz), location updates (latitude and longitude, heading, speed and accuracy) for navigation context, and a short text context (the active mode, your language and voice settings, and a one-line weather summary). Google returns speech and text. This is how the live assistant works.
  • How Google handles it. Google processes this data under the Gemini API terms you accepted when you created your key. On Google's unpaid (free) tier, Google says it may use submitted content and responses to improve its products, and that human reviewers may read them. See the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. Cinderspire never receives any of it.
  • Your API key, when you tap TEST. Tapping TEST on the key screen sends your key to Google once to check it. Your key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Google.
  • Your coordinates, for the weather. Once per app launch, when the app first gets your location, it sends your device's coordinates to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to fetch the current weather. No key or account is used.
  • Your SOS text, only if you send it. When you use SOS with an emergency contact saved, the emergency text — with your location and a Google Maps link, when LiveSight has a location fix — is prepared in Apple's Messages app. It is sent only if you tap Send there. LiveSight does not send it or keep it.
  • “Share location”. The voice command “share location” copies a Google Maps link of your current position to the clipboard. Nothing is sent.
  • What stays on your device. Your Gemini API key, your emergency contacts (the names and phone numbers you type in), your settings and the Journal are stored only on your device. The Journal is a record of what LiveSight announced, kept for 90 days (up to 4,000 entries). It is never uploaded; it leaves your device only if you choose to share or export it through the iOS share sheet.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • an account, sign-up or login — there is none
  • your name or email address
  • advertising data or an advertising identifier — the app shows no ads
  • tracking — the app does not track you
  • analytics — the app includes no analytics service
  • crash reports — the app includes no crash-reporting service
  • your photo library — the app never opens it or saves to it
  • your contacts book — emergency contacts are only the ones you type in
  • payment card details
  • anything on a server of ours — there is no developer server

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. What LiveSight stores stays on your device, and nothing it sends goes to us — so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

LiveSight is free and sells nothing, so it handles no payments. If you turn on Google's paid tier for your own key, that is between you and Google.

Third-party services

These are the only outside services the app talks to. Each has its own terms or privacy policy.

  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC) — Receives camera frames, microphone audio, location updates and a short text context, sent with your own API key only while a session is running, and returns speech and text. It also checks your key when you tap TEST. Gemini API terms and Google privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) — Receives your device's coordinates once per app launch and returns the current weather. Open-Meteo terms.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Permissions. LiveSight uses:

  • Camera — the live view the assistant looks at.
  • Microphone — your voice conversation with the assistant.
  • Location, while using the app — navigation context, the weather and the SOS text.
  • Motion and orientation, if enabled — the compass and fall detection.

You can turn Camera, Microphone and Location off at any time in iOS Settings → LiveSight.

Deleting your data.

  • In LiveSight, Settings → Privacy → Delete All My Data resets the app and deletes your data from it.
  • Journal → Delete the Journal deletes the whole Journal at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • To stop your Gemini API key from working, delete it in Google AI Studio. In LiveSight, Settings → API clears it from the app.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. LiveSight sends nothing to us, so the honest answer is usually that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly. For what you sent to Google through the Gemini API, Google's terms and privacy policy linked above apply.

Children

LiveSight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

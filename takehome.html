What the app does

Take-Home Pay UK is an offline calculator that turns a UK salary or hourly rate into take-home pay: income tax for England, Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland, employee National Insurance, student loan Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5 and the postgraduate loan, pension (relief at source, net pay or salary sacrifice) and tax codes. See pay by year, month, 4 weeks, week, day and hour, convert hourly pay to annual pay, work out pro rata pay, and see what you keep from a pay rise, bonus or overtime.

The core calculator is free, with no ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks salary comparison, payslip check, unlimited saved scenarios and PDF/CSV export. All results are estimates; your payslip and HMRC decide what is actually deducted.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app, tap Settings (gear icon), then Restore Purchases — or open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases there.
  3. You will see “Pro restored.” when it is back.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would rather fix it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every calculation runs on your iPhone, and the tax rates are built into the app, so it works offline. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

Which tax year and rates does it use?

2026/27 and 2025/26, using the rates and thresholds published on gov.uk: Personal Allowance, England, Wales and Northern Ireland bands, Scottish bands, employee Class 1 National Insurance, and student and postgraduate loan thresholds. You choose the tax year in Tax details. Rates are updated with app updates.

Can I rely on the results?

The app is an independent calculator that gives estimates. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HMRC or any government body. Your payslip and HMRC decide what is actually deducted; check your payslip, tax code or an adviser before relying on a result. Payslips work tax out month by month, so small differences are normal.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account, and the iPhone app collects no data. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the figures you entered and what you expected — a screenshot helps.

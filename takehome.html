What the app does

Payslip Preview shows what each of the twelve pay runs of a UK tax year will look like before payday. Pick a month and see gross pay, income tax with your tax code, employee National Insurance, student loan and postgraduate loan, pension and net pay. Tax is worked out month by month with the cumulative PAYE method, so a bonus or a pay change moves the months around it. Add a one-off payment or a pay change to any month, read the whole year as a ledger, and check a real payslip line by line against the preview.

The app also has a pension lab (what a contribution really costs under salary sacrifice, net pay and relief at source, the 60% zone and the Child Benefit charge), a student loan path (when a loan clears or is written off), a tax-year comparison with a frozen-threshold projection, rise, bonus and overtime, hourly and pro rata pay, and saved plans.

Everything above is free, with no ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds PDF and CSV export of the year, unlimited saved plans, plan-against-plan comparison, and backup and restore. All results are estimates; your payslip and HMRC decide what is actually deducted. The app is independent and is not connected to HMRC.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app, tap Tools, then Settings and backup and Restore Purchases — or open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases there.
  3. You will see “Pro is back on this device.” when it is back.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would rather fix it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every calculation runs on your iPhone or iPad, and the tax rates are built into the app, so it works offline. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

Which tax year and rates does it use?

2026/27 and 2025/26, using the rates and thresholds published on gov.uk: Personal Allowance, England, Wales and Northern Ireland bands, Scottish bands, employee Class 1 National Insurance, and student and postgraduate loan thresholds. You choose the tax year in Your pay details. Rates are updated with app updates.

Can I rely on the results?

The app is an independent calculator that gives estimates. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HMRC or any government body. Your payslip and HMRC decide what is actually deducted; check your payslip, tax code or an adviser before relying on a result. The preview follows the cumulative method payroll uses, but employers have their own pay dates and rounding, so small differences are normal.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

With Pro: tap Tools, then Settings and backup, then Copy (or Save file). Paste or keep the text somewhere safe. On the new phone choose Restore from copied backup after copying that text. The backup holds your pay details and saved plans and nothing else.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. There is no account, and the app collects no data. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the figures you entered and what you expected — a screenshot helps.

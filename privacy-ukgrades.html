Privacy Policy

Q

Quillmark: Degree & GCSE

A study journal for UK students. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app Quillmark only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Everything Quillmark does runs on your iPhone or iPad. Your modules, assessments, marks, deadlines, subjects, mock results, offers and saved checks stay on your device. The app shows no ads, has no analytics or crash-reporting service and sends nothing about you to us.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything that is stored or sent anywhere.

  • What you type stays on the device. Modules, credits, marks, assessments, deadlines, subjects, mock results, grade boundaries, offers, qualifications, saved checks and your look and reminder settings are stored inside the app on your device. There is no cloud sync, so we cannot see them. If you use iCloud Backup for your device, Apple includes the app’s data in that backup under your own Apple account.
  • Reminders. If you switch a reminder on, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered by your phone; nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Whether Quillmark Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • Sharing and exports. A result card image, a PDF study record, a CSV file or a backup is created on your device and handed to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent to us. Backup text you copy is placed on your clipboard and stays there until you paste or replace it.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • contacts, photos or files (the app does not ask for access to them)
  • location
  • your marks, grades, deadlines or offers
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell. On the App Store this app is labelled “Data Not Collected”.

Payments

Quillmark Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple on the App Store and charged to your Apple ID. We never receive or store your payment details — Apple only tells the app whether the purchase is active.

Third-party services

None, apart from Apple’s App Store for the optional purchase (Apple privacy policy).

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • You can edit or delete any plan, module, deadline, subject, mock, offer or saved check inside the app at any time, and “Erase everything” in the study desk removes all of it at once.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • You can turn off notifications for the app in iOS Settings at any time.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

The app is made for students (GCSE, A level and university) and contains nothing unsuitable for children. It has no accounts, no chat, no ads and no links that collect data, so there is nothing to collect from a child either. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — the app collects none.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example a new feature that sends something online — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

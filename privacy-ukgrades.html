Privacy Policy

U

Degree & Grade Calculator UK

Degree class from module marks, GCSE and A level grades from your boundaries, and tariff points. This policy covers the iPhone app (shown as “UK Grades” on your home screen) only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version

Every calculation runs on your iPhone. Your modules, marks, grade boundaries, tariff lines and saved scenarios stay on your device. The app shows no ads, has no analytics or crash-reporting service and sends nothing about you to us.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything that is stored or sent anywhere.

  • What you type stays on the device. Modules, credits, marks, grade boundaries, qualifications, saved scenarios and your theme setting are stored inside the app on your iPhone. There is no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Whether UK Grades Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • CSV export (Pro). When you export scenarios, a CSV file is handed to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Links you tap. “Privacy policy” and “Support” in Settings open this website in your browser; “Contact” opens your email app.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • contacts, photos or files
  • location
  • your marks, grades or saved scenarios
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell. On the App Store this app is labelled “Data Not Collected”.

Payments

UK Grades Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple on the App Store and charged to your Apple ID. We never receive or store your payment details — Apple only tells the app whether the purchase is active.

Third-party services

None, apart from Apple’s App Store for the optional purchase (Apple privacy policy).

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • You can rename or delete any saved scenario inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over (GCSE, A level and university students) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — the app collects none.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example a new feature that sends something online — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

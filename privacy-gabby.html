Gabby: Habit Tracker & Streak

Habits, streaks, journal, water tracker, focus timer and widgets. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

Your habits, streaks, journal entries and mood logs stay on your device. The only things that leave it are ad requests and crash reports. Gabby does not read Apple Health or Google Health Connect data.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing you type. Habits, streaks, journal entries, mood logs, water intake and focus sessions are stored on your device, inside the app. There is no developer-run server and no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Sentry receives a crash stack trace along with your device model, OS version and app version. Your journal and habit content is not included.
  • Ad requests. Google AdMob receives standard technical data such as device type, coarse region and IP address as part of serving any ad in the free version. Gabby uses AdMob's default ad settings, which may include personalised ads; you can limit this with your device's ad settings, described below.
  • Whether Premium is active. Apple or Google tells the app whether your entitlement is active.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • precise location
  • photos
  • browsing history
  • payment card details
  • Apple Health or Google Health Connect data — Gabby does not read from either

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple on the App Store or by Google on Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details — the store only tells the app whether your entitlement is active.

Third-party services

These are the only outside services the app talks to. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob — Receives ad requests, including device type, coarse region and IP address. Google AdMob privacy policy.
  • Sentry — Receives crash stack traces, device model, OS version and app version when the app crashes. Sentry privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stored on your device.
  • Where the app offers a reset or delete option in its settings, using it clears the data you chose to remove.
  • You can delete individual habits and journal entries inside the app at any time.

Limiting ad tracking.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Gabby is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

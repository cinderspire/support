What the app does

Work Hours & Days UK is an offline calculator for UK working time: hours worked across shifts (including overnight shifts and breaks), working days between two dates, adding or subtracting working days, statutory annual leave (including part-time, irregular hours and term-time), UK bank holidays for England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, ISO and PAYE tax week numbers, and date maths.

The app is free, with no ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited saved results and CSV export.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app, tap Settings (gear icon), then Restore Purchases — or open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases there.
  3. You will see “Pro restored.” when it is back.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would rather fix it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every calculation runs on your iPhone, and the UK bank holiday dates are built into the app, so it works offline. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

Which bank holidays are included?

The public bank holiday dates for England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, including substitute days, for the years shown on the Bank holidays screen. The dates are updated with app updates. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Can I rely on the results?

The app is an independent calculator for guidance. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body. Annual leave and working-time rules can depend on your contract — check your contract or an adviser before relying on a result.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account, and the iPhone app collects no data. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the inputs you used and what you expected — a screenshot helps.

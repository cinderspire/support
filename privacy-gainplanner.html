Gain Planner privacy policy

Published by Cinderspire. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: Gain Planner has no account and no tracking. Your details, meal plan, shopping ticks and weigh-ins stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • The details you enter: age, height, weight, activity level, pace, protein level and food choices (vegetarian, foods to leave out).
  • Your meal plan, which items you ticked as eaten, your shopping-list ticks and your weigh-ins (date and weight).
  • Settings such as units.

You can delete all of it at any time in You > Settings > Erase all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

What leaves your device

  • Promo card request. The app may ask a small service for a card from the makers of the app. The request contains the app's identifier, the operating system, a cluster name (lifestyle) and whether the device is a phone or tablet. It contains no name, no email, no advertising identifier and none of the details above. Taps on a card are counted by that service.
  • Links and sharing. Links to guides and resources open in your browser, and their own policies apply. When you share a shopping list, the text goes only to the app you choose.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photos.

Health information

Gain Planner gives general estimates for healthy adults. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. If you are underweight due to illness, pregnant, under 18, or have an eating disorder, talk to a doctor first. The numbers you enter never leave your device.

Children

Gain Planner is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data sources

Food values come from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy), a public-domain data set bundled in the app. Gain Planner is not affiliated with any government body.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: support@playtools.top.

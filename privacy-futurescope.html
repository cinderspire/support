Privacy Policy

F

FutureScope: Future Timeline

A browsable timeline of 505 sourced forecasts for 2025–2048. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

The forecast content ships inside the app, so reading it does not call us at all. The only thing that leaves your device is an ad request in the free version. There is no crash-reporting SDK and no account.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing about what you read. The forecast content ships inside the app, so browsing it requires no network call to us. Your bookmarks and settings are stored on your device.
  • Ad requests. Google AdMob receives standard technical data such as device type, coarse region and IP address as part of serving any ad in the free version. Ads are requested as non-personalized. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form may appear before the first ad; you can change your choice later in About ▸ Ad privacy choices. Buying Remove Ads (a one-time purchase) or FutureScope Pro (a subscription on iPhone and iPad) stops the ads.
  • Whether Remove Ads or FutureScope Pro is active. Apple or Google tells the app whether your purchase or subscription is active, and the app keeps that answer on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • precise location
  • photos
  • browsing history
  • payment card details
  • crash reports — the app includes no crash-reporting SDK

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple on the App Store or by Google on Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details — the store only tells the app whether your purchase is active.

Third-party services

These are the only outside services the app talks to. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob — Receives ad requests, including device type, coarse region and IP address. Google AdMob privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stored on your device.
  • Where the app offers a reset or delete option in its settings, using it clears the data you chose to remove.

Limiting ad tracking.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

FutureScope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

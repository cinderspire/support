Market Sim

A stock market game with invented companies and virtual money.

Learn how markets work: trade 30 fictional companies and 6 funds, read candlestick charts, finish 26 missions and play seasons. No real money, no real tickers, no account.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real trading?
No. All money is virtual and all companies are invented. Nothing in the game is financial advice.

Why did my season end?
A Quick season lasts 63 trading days and a Full year lasts 252. You can also end a season early in Portfolio > Seasons.

Can I replay the same market?
Yes. Every season shows its market seed on the Seasons screen. Enter it under "Replay a specific market" when you start a new season.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your device. Reset it in Settings > Reset all progress.

How do limit orders work?
A limit order waits until a day's price range reaches your price. Cash or shares are held for you until it fills or you cancel it.

What do trades cost?
0.05% of the trade value, at least $0.50 and at most $9.99, plus the spread between the bid and the ask.

Privacy policy

Post Preview Studio by Cinderspire

Summary

Post Preview Studio works on your device. Your photos, plans, drafts and hashtag sets are stored only on the device. We do not run user accounts, we do not collect personal data and we do not use advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select; it cannot browse your library. Photos are processed on the device to preview, crop and split them. Exported images are written to your photo library or handed to the share sheet only when you tap Save or Share. Exported files contain no location data and no watermark.

Stored on your device

  • Planned posts: small copies of the photos you add to the plan, notes and dates.
  • Caption drafts and hashtag sets you write.
  • Display choices such as the grid ratio.

This data is removed when you delete the app. It is not uploaded or backed up by the app.

Network requests

The app may download a small public configuration file from a static website and may request one house promotion card about the publisher's own websites or apps. These requests contain the app identifier, the operating system, a content group and a device class (phone or tablet). They contain no personal data, no advertising identifier and no photos. Tapping a promotion card opens your browser or the store. Cards are not personalised.

Permissions

The app asks to add photos to your library only when you save an export. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Delete a plan item, a draft or a hashtag set inside the app, or remove all data by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to export or erase on our side.

Privacy Policy

P

Payslip Preview

A month-by-month payslip preview for UK employees: income tax, National Insurance, student loan and pension for every pay run of the tax year. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Everything you enter — pay, hours, tax code, loan and pension choices, one-off payments and saved plans — stays on your iPhone or iPad. The app collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content. Calculations and the bundled tax rates work offline.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything the app keeps or hands on.

  • What you type stays on the device. Pay figures, hours, tax year, region, tax code, student loan and pension choices, the one-off payment and pay change you set, appearance and up to 3 saved plans (unlimited with Pro) are stored inside the app on your device. There is no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time Pro purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • Export and sharing. When you share a result as text, or export a PDF, CSV or backup file (Pro), it is handed to the app you choose in the share sheet. A copied backup goes to the system clipboard, which you control. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Links you tap. “Privacy policy” and “Help and contact” in Settings open pages in your browser; the email address opens your email app.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the iPhone app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • contacts, photos or files
  • location
  • your pay, tax details or saved plans
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details — Apple only tells the app whether Pro is unlocked.

Third-party services

The iPhone app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

  • Apple App Store — handles the optional Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. Apple privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • You can delete any saved plan in Saved plans, or use “Erase my details and plans” in Settings and backup to remove everything at once.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Payslip Preview is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

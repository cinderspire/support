Last updated: 27 August 2026

True of all of our apps

Whichever app you use, these things hold.

  • No account, no sign-up, no login. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app.
  • Your content stays on your device. There is no developer-run server and no cloud sync, so we cannot see what you create.
  • We never see your payment details. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, who only tell the app whether an entitlement is active.
  • Uninstalling deletes the data. Apps also offer an in-app reset or delete where one is present.
  • Not for children under 13. Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

The per-app policies below add the specifics: which ad, crash-reporting or AI services each app talks to, and precisely what those services receive.

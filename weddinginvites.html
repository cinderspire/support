Wedding Invites Studio

Design invitations, save the dates, reply cards and menus, find the right wording, and keep track of your guest list, all on your phone or tablet.

Need help? Email support@playtools.top. Please mention your device and app version (More tab, bottom of the screen).

Frequently asked questions

Where are my designs and guest list stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy. If you delete the app, the data is deleted too, so export your guest list as a CSV file to keep a backup.

How do I print an invitation?

Tap Export, choose the size and PDF, switch on bleed if your printer asks for it, then Share and pick Files, Mail or your printer app. PDF pages are exactly the card size and are rendered at 300 dpi.

Which sizes are available?

5 x 7 inch, A5 and A6, with or without bleed, plus a 1080 x 1920 phone-sized image to share by message.

Why does my text look smaller on some cards?

Text always shrinks to fit the card so nothing is cut off. Use the Text size slider in the Look tab, or shorten a line, to make it larger again.

What does [text in square brackets] mean?

Wording ideas contain placeholders such as [Host surname] for you to replace. The export sheet warns you if any are left on the card.

How do reminders work?

In Guests, open the menu and choose Reminder messages. Pick a message and tap Text or Email beside a guest. Your own Messages or Mail app opens with the text filled in. Nothing is sent automatically.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection except the web links in the More tab.

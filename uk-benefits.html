Questions

Is this an official government service?

No. Benefits Checker UK is an independent app. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any UK government department. Figures come from public information on GOV.UK and are estimates only.

How accurate is it?

It follows the published 2026/27 rates and rules and shows its working, but it gives estimates, not decisions. Always confirm with the official service before you rely on a figure.

Does it need an account or my name?

No. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

Which benefits does it estimate?

Universal Credit (including the benefit cap), Child Benefit and its tax charge, Carer's Allowance, Attendance Allowance and Pension Credit's Guarantee Credit.

What does it not cover?

Scottish and Northern Ireland benefits, older benefits such as Housing Benefit and tax credits, Council Tax Reduction, Personal Independence Payment amounts, Savings Credit, help with mortgage interest and students.

Is the two-child limit included?

The limit ended on 6 April 2026, so every child counts.

Where do the numbers come from?

From GOV.UK: the Benefit and pension rates 2026 to 2027 publication and the guidance pages linked in the app's Rates and sources screen.

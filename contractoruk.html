Contractor Pay UK

Turn a day rate into take-home pay as a limited company, through an umbrella or as a sole trader, and keep the dates that matter.

Not affiliated with any government body. Estimates only, not tax advice. Official sources: gov.uk income tax, dividends, corporation tax.

Questions

Is this tax advice?

No. It gives estimates from the published rates. Check important decisions with an accountant.

Which tax years are covered?

2026/27 (current) and 2025/26. Rates come from gov.uk and were checked on 8 November 2026.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your phone.

Why does the best salary change with my day rate?

The app tries every whole-pound salary the company can afford and keeps the one that leaves you the most. The answer depends on your profit.

Does it cover Scottish tax bands, student loans or benefits in kind?

Not in this version.

How do reminders work?

Turn on the bell on a date in the Dates tab. Your phone schedules a notification for 9am UK time, a few days before. No server is involved.

What LiveSight does

LiveSight is a live voice assistant for blind and low-vision people, on iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 or later). While a session is running, it streams your camera and microphone to a live AI model from Google, which looks at the camera view and talks back to you. LiveSight adds haptic feedback, voice commands, a compass, the current weather, battery status and an emergency SOS button.

You choose a mode at the bottom of the screen:

  • Navigation mode warns you about obstacles, with a clock direction and a distance, and uses haptic patterns to show how urgent a warning is.
  • Traffic mode tells you the colour of the traffic light it sees. Always confirm with your cane, guide dog or other cues before crossing.
  • Colors mode describes the colour, pattern and material of clothing and objects.
  • Expiration mode reads expiry dates on packaging and says whether a product is expired, expiring soon or fresh.
  • Explore mode describes your surroundings.

LiveSight is an aid, not a replacement for a white cane, a guide dog, orientation and mobility training, or emergency services. The AI can be wrong or miss things.

You need your own free Google Gemini API key. LiveSight has no AI server of its own and no built-in key. The first time you open the app, it explains how to create a free key in Google AI Studio — see the questions below.

The assistant can speak English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Arabic, Chinese or Japanese, in a voice you choose. The app's own screens are in English.

LiveSight also keeps a Journal on your device: a record of everything it announced. All five modes, SOS and today's Journal entries are free, with no ads and no account. LiveSight Premium is an optional subscription that adds the Journal's earlier days and lets you share or export the Journal.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do I get a Google Gemini API key, and where do I enter it?

LiveSight has no AI server and no built-in key, so it uses your own key from Google. The key is free: you need a Google account, but no credit card. The first time you open LiveSight, the setup screens explain these steps:

  1. Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey) and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create an API key and copy it.
  3. Paste the key into LiveSight.
  4. Tap TEST to check that the key works.

To test, change or clear your key later, open Settings (the gear icon) → API in LiveSight.

Your key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Google. On Google's free tier, Google's own rate limits apply, and LiveSight shows them in the app. Any usage or charges on your key are between you and Google — LiveSight never charges for what the camera looks at.

The live session stops or will not start — what should I check?

Check these in order:

  1. Your key. In LiveSight, open Settings → API and tap TEST. If the test fails, paste a working key.
  2. Google's limit. If your key has reached Google's limit, the session cannot start or continue until the key works again. You can wait for the limit to reset, or use another key.
  3. Your internet connection. The live session talks to Google over the internet, so make sure you are connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  4. Permissions. Open the iOS Settings app → LiveSight and check that Camera and Microphone are allowed. Location (While Using the App) is used for navigation context, the weather and the SOS text.

If it still does not work, fully close LiveSight and open it again. Then email us and tell us what happens when you try to start a session.

How does the emergency SOS button work?

Hold the red SOS button for 3 seconds. A 10-second countdown starts, with a Cancel button. When the countdown ends:

  • If you have saved at least one emergency contact, LiveSight opens Apple's Messages app with a ready-made emergency text addressed to your primary contact (or to your first contact, if none is marked primary). When LiveSight has a location fix, the text includes your current location and a Google Maps link. Nothing is sent until you tap Send in Messages.
  • If no contact is saved, LiveSight tells you that nobody was contacted and to call your local emergency number.

SOS works even when a LiveSight session is not running. Your emergency contacts — a name and a phone number for each — are the ones you type into LiveSight yourself, and they are stored only on your device. LiveSight does not read the contacts on your iPhone or iPad.

LiveSight never calls emergency services and never sends a message by itself. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What is free, and what does LiveSight Premium add?

Free, with or without Premium: all five modes, voice commands, haptics, the compass, the weather, SOS and emergency contacts, and today's Journal entries. There are no ads and no account.

LiveSight Premium adds two things:

  • the Journal's earlier days — the Journal is a record, kept on your device, of everything LiveSight announced, and it keeps entries for 90 days (up to 4,000 entries);
  • sharing or exporting the Journal through the iOS share sheet, only when you choose to.

To see the plans, open Settings (the gear icon) → General → LiveSight Premium → See LiveSight Premium plans and prices, or open the Journal and tap See LiveSight Premium. Premium is sold only on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad.

How do I restore a purchase?

LiveSight Premium is tied to the Apple ID that paid for it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the device with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open LiveSight and go to Settings → General, or open the Premium plans screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Fully close the app and open it again.

If it still does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

LiveSight Premium is managed by Apple, not inside LiveSight, so you manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

On iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select the LiveSight subscription, then tap Cancel Subscription.

While you are subscribed, the Manage Subscription button in LiveSight also opens Apple's subscription page. You can also go straight there in a browser: Manage your Apple subscriptions.

Two things worth knowing. Cancelling stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for, and to avoid the next charge you need to cancel at least 24 hours before that period ends. And deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: you have to cancel it in your Apple ID settings, or it keeps renewing.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and we cannot issue or reverse a payment — only Apple can do that.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If you are asking for a refund because something is broken, please email us as well. We would much rather fix the problem — and if we can, you may not need the refund at all.

Where is my data stored, and what leaves my device?

Your API key, emergency contacts, settings and Journal are stored on your device, inside the app. There is no account to create and no password to remember. The Journal is never uploaded; it leaves your device only if you choose to share or export it.

While a session is running, LiveSight sends camera frames, microphone audio, your location and a short text context directly from your device to Google, using your own key — that is how the assistant sees and answers. To show the weather, it sends your coordinates to Open-Meteo once per launch. LiveSight sends nothing to us, and there is no developer server.

The LiveSight privacy policy has every detail, including how to delete your data.

The app crashes, or a feature does not work — what should I do?

Three quick things fix most of it:

  1. Update to the latest version of LiveSight in the App Store — the fix may already be out.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your device.
  3. Check that your device has enough free storage.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top and include:

  • Your device model (for example iPhone 13 or iPad Air).
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The app version, if you can find it.
  • Which mode you were using, and what you did or said just before it went wrong.

That is usually enough for us to reproduce the problem straight away. Please do not include your API key.

I bought Premium but it is not unlocked

Open LiveSight, go to Settings → General and tap Restore Purchases, then fully close and reopen the app. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium.

If Premium is still locked, email us and mention which plan you bought and roughly when — we will take it from there.

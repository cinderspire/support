Frequently asked questions

How are reaction times measured?

From the frame in which the target is drawn to the moment your touch registers, both read from the same system clock. Your screen and touch latency are included, so numbers are higher than on a desktop mouse test. Compare yourself with yourself on the same device.

Why is a drill the same size on every device?

Targets are sized in millimetres and converted with the standard point density of your device, so a phone and a tablet show the same physical size. The conversion is an estimate because the operating system does not report the exact screen density.

What is a false start in Reflex?

Tapping before the flash, or less than 100 milliseconds after it (too fast to be a reaction). The trial restarts with a new random wait and costs points.

How does the daily challenge work?

Every calendar day has one seeded drill, the same for everyone. Finish it to extend your streak. Replaying it is allowed but does not count twice.

Where do the sensitivity values come from?

The tool uses published or widely measured yaw values (degrees of turn per mouse count). They are listed with their sources in the app under Sensitivity and About. Check your own 360 turn in game for precision. Game names are labels only; FPSAim is not affiliated with those games.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Sessions and settings stay on the device. See the privacy policy.

I lost my progress.

Progress lives only on the device and is removed when the app is deleted or when you use Reset all progress. There is no account to restore it from.

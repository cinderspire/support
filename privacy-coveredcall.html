CCovered Call Calc

Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Published by Cinderspire.

Short version. Covered Call Calc has no account, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and no advertising identifiers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The positions you log, the numbers you type into the calculators, your settings and your reminders are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive them and cannot read them. Uninstalling the app or using Settings > Erase all data removes them. The CSV export is created only when you ask for it and goes where you send it through the system share sheet.

The one network request the app makes

The app may ask a small promotion service for a card to show in a slot at the bottom of some screens, and it fetches a small configuration file that tells it where that service is. The request contains the app identifier, the operating system (iOS or Android), the app's category and whether the device is a phone or tablet. It contains no advertising identifier, device identifier, account, location or content you entered. If you tap a card, your browser opens a link on our redirect service, which counts the tap. Ordinary server logs (IP address and time) may be kept briefly by the hosting provider for security. Without a network connection the app works fully and simply shows no card.

Links

Learn guides and the About screen link to coveredcallcalculator.net and to public sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and are opened in your browser.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you switch them on and the system asks for permission. No notification content is sent to us.

Children

The app is an options-education tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that applies). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Not advice

Educational calculator. Not investment advice. Options involve risk and are not suitable for all investors. Tax notes are general and for US taxpayers only; consult a tax professional.

Changes and contact

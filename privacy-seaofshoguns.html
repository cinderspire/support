Privacy Policy

S

Sea of Shoguns: Naval Strategy

A samurai-era naval strategy game for one player that works fully offline. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

Sea of Shoguns is a single-player game. It works fully offline and sends nothing anywhere. Your saved games and settings stay on your device. The game shows no ads, offers no purchases, has no account and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to play.

What the game handles

Nothing is sent to us. Here is everything the game stores, and where.

  • Saved games. Your campaign progress (season, provinces, fleets) and your skirmish setup are saved automatically on your device.
  • Settings. Language, music and sound volume, vibration, graphics quality, battle speed, camera sensitivity and which tutorial hints you have seen are stored on your device only.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the game shows no ads
  • your real name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • location
  • photos, camera or microphone — the game uses none of them
  • payment card details
  • purchase information — this version offers no in-app purchases
  • analytics or usage statistics — the game includes no analytics SDK
  • crash reports — the game includes no crash-reporting SDK

There is no account, no sign-up and no login, and no developer-run server. We have nothing about you to look at, hand over or sell.

Purchases

This version offers no in-app purchases, so the game handles no payment or purchase information at all.

Your choices and rights

Your data is on your device, so you are the one holding it.

  • The game makes no internet connection while you play.
  • Starting a new campaign replaces your old save.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device.

Asking us anything. Email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request, including a request to access or delete data. We hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, so in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Sea of Shoguns: Naval Strategy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, such as a new online feature, we update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

Privacy policy

Library Hours Now for iPhone and iPad. Operated by Cinderspire. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: no account, no tracking, no advertising identifiers. Your location, saved libraries and study log stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Saved libraries, reminder choices, settings, the study timer state and your study log are stored locally on your phone. They are never uploaded and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

Location is optional. The app asks for your approximate location only after you tap "Use my location", uses it on your device to sort libraries by distance, keeps it in memory and does not store it or send it anywhere. You can use the app fully by searching for a city instead.

Notifications

If you switch on a closing reminder or start the study timer, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are not push notifications and no message is sent from a server.

Network requests

  • Library list. The app downloads a public file (libraries.json, hosted on GitHub Pages) to refresh opening hours, at most every few hours. The request contains no identifier and no location. As with any web request, the hosting provider sees the IP address of the request.
  • Optional house promotions. The app may ask our own promotion service for a small card (for example, our website). The request contains the app identifier, the platform, a category name and a device class (phone or tablet). It contains no advertising identifier, no location and no personal data. Taps on a promotion are counted.
  • Links you tap. Directions, phone, email and website buttons open other apps or sites; their privacy policies apply there.

Data sources

Opening hours come mostly from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; about 25,000 libraries, community data) and partly from libraryhours24.com. Hours can change without notice; please check with the library.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can deny location and notification permissions in your device settings and the app keeps working. You can clear your study log inside the app (More, Study). Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to export or delete on our side.

Questions

Does Homebuy UK need an account?
No. It works offline and keeps your figures on your device.
Which rates does it use?
The rates in force on your completion date for England and Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales, taken from the official pages and checked on 8 November 2026. Every result shows the date and a link to the official page.
Is it financial or tax advice?
No. It gives estimates only. Check with a solicitor, adviser or lender before you commit.
Is it affiliated with a government body or lender?
No. Homebuy UK is independent and not affiliated with any government body or lender.
How do I report a wrong figure?
Email support@playtools.top with the price, region, date and what you expected, and we will check it against the official page.

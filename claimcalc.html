ClaimCalc: Claim Organizer

A private organizer for an accident or injury claim.

Keep dates, costs, receipts, photos and a symptom journal in one private file on your phone, and share a PDF summary with your own lawyer or insurer. Everything stays on your device.

Not legal advice. ClaimCalc does not give legal advice, find lawyers or review claims. Estimates are educational ranges, and filing periods are general references with exceptions. Talk to a licensed attorney. Not affiliated with any government body or insurance company.

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. ClaimCalc is not a law firm and does not give legal advice, find lawyers or review claims. The estimate shows educational ranges from two common worksheets, and every case differs. A licensed attorney can advise on your situation.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. You can erase everything in Settings.

How is mileage calculated?

Miles times a rate you can edit, rounded to the cent. The starting rate is the 2026 federal standard medical mileage rate (23.5 cents per mile for July to December 2026).

What does the date reference show?

A general personal injury filing period for the state you choose, counted from the incident date, with the statute citation and the date the table was last checked. Exceptions apply, and claims involving public bodies often have much shorter notice rules. Confirm with an attorney.

Can I send my file to my lawyer or insurer?

Yes. Export a PDF summary and use the share sheet to send it wherever you choose.

Which platforms?

iPhone and Android phones.

What Longbreak does

Longbreak is a leave and bank holiday planner for people who work in the UK. It keeps everything on one calendar and does the sums for you.

  • Calendar. Month and year views with bank holidays, booked leave and rota shifts. Tap a day to book leave or log a shift.
  • Leave balance. Set your allowance, whether bank holidays are included, carry-over and the start of your leave year. Book whole days, half days, time off in lieu or unpaid leave; weekends, bank holidays and days you do not work are skipped.
  • Bridge days. Finds the breaks worth taking in the next twelve months, such as “take 4 days, get 10 off”, using your work pattern, rota and region.
  • Rota. Repeating shift patterns (4 on 4 off, 2-2-3, continental and more, or your own cycle of up to 28 days) laid over the calendar.
  • Shift diary. Log shifts and breaks, see the week against your contract hours, and with Pro see month totals, overtime and a gross pay estimate.
  • Bank holidays. England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, including substitute days.
  • Reminders. Optional notifications on your phone before leave starts, before a bank holiday, and before your leave year ends. The notification permission is requested only when you switch a reminder on.
  • Toolbox. Working days between dates, adding or taking away time, ISO and tax week numbers, hours worked and statutory holiday entitlement.
  • Calendar file and backup. Share booked leave as an .ics file and back up or restore the whole planner.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open Longbreak, open the menu (top left), choose Longbreak Pro, then tap Restore Purchases.
  3. You will see “Your Pro purchase is back.” when it is restored.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

How is my leave balance worked out?

Your allowance is the days a year your contract gives you plus anything carried over. If you tell Longbreak that bank holidays are included, the bank holidays that fall on your working days come out of it. Every booked working day then counts against what is left: a full day is 1, a morning or afternoon is 0.5, and time off in lieu or unpaid leave do not touch your allowance. If you joined part way through the leave year, the allowance is pro-rated.

How does the bridge-day finder choose?

It looks at the next twelve months for runs of days off that contain a bank holiday, need no more days of leave than you choose, and give at least three more days off than the leave they cost. Weekends, bank holidays, days you do not work (or your rota days off) and leave you have already booked count as free. For each stretch of the calendar it shows the longest run that fits.

Why does Longbreak ask about notifications?

Only if you switch on a reminder. The reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in the app or in the iPhone Settings app.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone and the bank holiday dates are built into the app. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

Which bank holidays are included?

The public bank holiday lists for England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, including substitute days, from 2019. Years the bundled list does not cover use the standard rules and are marked as estimates. Dates are updated with app updates. Where is my data stored, and how do I move it?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and the app collects no data. Use Calendar & backup in the menu to save a backup file and, on a new phone, to paste it back with Restore. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Can I rely on the results?

Longbreak is an independent planner. It is not connected to the UK government or to any employer. Holiday balances and suggestions are guidance; your contract and your employer decide what you are owed.

Something does not work. What should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Gaugeful: Fuel & Trip Costs

A running-cost diary for the cars you actually drive: garage, fill-ups, EV charging, insights, reminders, multi-stop journeys and backup. Everything stays on your device.

What it does

Gaugeful is a running-cost diary for drivers in the UK and Germany. Keep a garage of several vehicles (petrol, diesel, hybrid, electric), log every fill-up or charge, and see what each mile or kilometre costs. Everything stays on your iPhone or iPad: no account, no server.

  • Garage. Several vehicles, each with its own log, units and tank or battery size. Switch vehicle from the header of every screen.
  • Fill-ups and charges. Real consumption from full tank to full tank; EV charging as home or public with your own home tariff; other costs such as service, MOT, tax, insurance, tyres, parking and tolls.
  • Insights. Monthly spend, cost per mile or kilometre, consumption trend with a rolling average, home versus public charging, and an EV-versus-petrol comparison.
  • Reminders. MOT / HU-TÜV, road tax, insurance and service by date or odometer reading, with optional local alerts.
  • Journeys. Multi-stop trip costing with tolls, parking and congestion charges, shareable as a card.
  • Backup. JSON backup and restore, CSV export and import.
  • Converter. mpg (UK and US), L/100 km, km/L, miles per kWh and kWh per 100 km.

You type every price yourself, so the app needs no internet connection. English and German, with UK and metric units.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Backups are files you control: JSON for a full backup, CSV for spreadsheets. The only in-app purchase is a one-time Pro unlock; the free app covers day-to-day use.

Gaugeful is an independent tool, not linked to any fuel company, car maker, charge network or government body. Prices are the ones you type.

Quillmark: Degree & GCSE

A study journal for UK students: modules and assessments, deadlines with reminders, GCSE and A level mock exams, and university offers checked against your predicted grades.

What the app does

Quillmark keeps your studies in one journal. It works offline, needs no account and runs on iPhone and iPad:

  • Degree: modules by year with credits, a final mark or several weighted assessments, your class on a seal-and-gauge, the average you need in everything not yet marked for the class you are aiming at, a “try a score” slider and a borderline checklist.
  • Planner: coursework and exam deadlines linked to modules, with optional reminders (local notifications) a week, three days or a day before, or on the day.
  • Exams: GCSE, Combined Science, AS and A level subjects with a target and predicted grade, a mock log with a trend line, grades from the boundaries you enter or a dated example table, and a grade finder.
  • Offers: type offers such as AAB, A*AA, ABB+, 128 points or DDM and compare them with your predicted grades and tariff points (2026 and 2027 entry).
  • Share and keep: a result card, a backup file or text block, and with Pro a PDF study record and a CSV.

Know where you stand

Add your modules year by year. Give each one a final mark or break it into assessments with weights, and enter the marks as they come back. A gauge with a wax seal shows your class, and the app works out the average you need in everything that is still to be marked for the class you are aiming at.

Never miss a date

Keep coursework and exam deadlines next to your modules. Switch on a reminder and your phone nudges you before the date. Reminders are local notifications; your phone asks for permission only when you switch the first one on.

Mock exams with a trend

Log every GCSE or A level mock with its mark and date. With the boundaries for your paper you get a grade for each mock, a trend line, and the gap to your target counted in marks.

Offers against predictions

Type a university offer the way it was written and see at once whether your predicted grades or tariff points meet it, and by how much.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device, and a backup file lets you move to a new phone. Quillmark is made by an independent developer, Mustafa Bilgiç (Cinderspire).

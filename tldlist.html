FAQ

Does TLD List sell domains?
No. It is a reference and lookup tool. It has no registrar links and no purchase flow.
How does the availability check work?
The app asks the registry's public RDAP service for the extension you pick. A 404 answer means the name has no registration record (likely available); a 200 answer means it is registered. Some extensions publish no RDAP service and show unknown.
Is likely available a guarantee?
No. It is not a reservation. Registries can hold premium or reserved names that still answer as unregistered, and a name can be taken right after you check.
Are the prices live?
No. They are indicative yearly ranges from tldlist.us with a date. Registrars set their own prices and renewals can differ.
What data leaves my device?
The domain name you check goes to that extension's RDAP server over HTTPS. See the privacy policy.

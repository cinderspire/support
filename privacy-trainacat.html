TrainACat privacy policy

Published by Cinderspire. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: TrainACat has no account and no tracking. The cats you add, their optional photos, your progress and your training log stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Your cats' names, approximate ages, coats and optional photos (chosen with the system photo picker and copied into the app's private storage).
  • Lesson progress, session logs (date, length, clicks, treats, how it went) and settings such as sound and theme.
  • Weekly reminder settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings > Erase all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

What leaves your device

  • Promo card request. The app may ask a small service for a card from the makers of the app. The request contains the app's bundle id, the operating system (iOS), a cluster name (lifestyle) and whether the device is a phone or tablet. It contains no name, no email, no advertising identifier and none of your cat data. Taps on a card are counted by that service.
  • Links. Links to trainacat.us or to other pages open in your browser. Their own policies apply.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone or camera. Photos are only the single picture you choose.

Notifications

If you switch on reminders, the app asks for notification permission. You can change it at any time in your device settings.

Children

TrainACat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Not veterinary advice

TrainACat shares general, reward-based training ideas. It does not give medical advice. If your cat seems unwell, please ask your vet.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: support@playtools.top.

Market Sim: Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Cinderspire. Contact: support@playtools.top

Short version. Market Sim has no accounts and no analytics SDK. Your seasons, scores, lessons and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

What stays on your device

Your current season, trades, leaderboard of past seasons, lesson and mission progress, badges and settings are saved in a file inside the app. Deleting the app, or using Settings > Reset all progress, removes it.

What the app sends

The app may ask a server for a small promotion card (for example a link to our websites). The request contains the app identifier, the operating system (iOS or Android), the device type (phone or tablet) and a cluster name. It contains no name, email, advertising identifier or device identifier. Tapping a card opens a link in your browser or store, and our server counts card views and clicks. This is product-interaction data used for advertising of our own apps and sites and for simple analytics; it is not linked to you and is never used to track you.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal data collection.
  • No advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics SDK.
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or notifications.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Links

Lessons link to guides on marketgames.us. Those pages are operated separately and have their own policies.

Children

Market Sim is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Game and education only

Market Sim uses virtual money and invented companies. It is not a broker and gives no financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@playtools.top.

TLD List: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. App publisher: Cinderspire. Contact: support@playtools.top

Summary

TLD List has no account, no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. Your favourites, watchlist and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is described below.

What stays on your device

  • Favourite extensions, the list of domain names you watch with their last results, the extensions you picked for checks, and your theme choice.
  • A cached copy of the extension list and of the IANA RDAP service list, so the app works offline.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We never receive it.

What is sent, and to whom

Domain names you check. When you check a name (or re-check a watched name), the app sends that domain name over HTTPS to the public RDAP server operated by the registry of its extension (for example the registry that runs .com), as listed in the IANA RDAP bootstrap file (for a few extensions that file lacks, such as .io or .de, the registry's own published RDAP server). This is how the lookup works. The request contains the domain name and a User-Agent that names the app, not you. We do not run these servers, we do not receive these requests, and the registry's own policy applies to what it logs. The app never uses WHOIS port 43 and never reads owner contact data from an answer.
List updates. About once a day the app downloads a newer extension list from GitHub Pages and the RDAP service list from iana.org. These are ordinary file downloads and carry no identifier.
Promo cards. The app may show a small card about our own websites or apps. To pick it, the app sends our promo server the app id, the platform (iOS or Android) and a cluster name, and the server counts the view or click. No device identifier, advertising ID or personal data is sent, and nothing is linked to you.

What we do not do

  • No tracking, no advertising identifier, no cross-app profiling, no sale of data.
  • No account, e-mail address, location, contacts or photos are requested.

Children

TLD List is a general audience tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Links and sources

Links open in your browser. Extension data comes from IANA; meanings, registries and typical prices come from tldlist.us (CC BY). TLD List is independent and is not affiliated with IANA, ICANN, any registry or registrar.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we update this page and its date. Questions or requests: support@playtools.top.

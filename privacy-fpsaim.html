FPSAim privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026. App: FPSAim (aim and reflex trainer). Publisher: Cinderspire. Contact: support@playtools.top

Summary

FPSAim has no account and no sign-in. Your training sessions, personal bests, streak and settings are stored only on your device. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers or tracking. Our server counts how often a promotional card is shown or opened, without identifying you. The app can fetch one small promotional card about our own websites and apps; that request contains no personal data.

What is stored on your device

  • Session results (drill, difficulty, score, accuracy, reaction times, date), daily-challenge completions and your preferences (mute, haptics, reduce flashing, target colour, your last sensitivity values).
  • Short generated sound files in the temporary folder.

This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it; "Reset all progress" in Settings deletes it immediately.

What the app sends over the network

  • A read of a small configuration file that tells the app whether to show promotional cards.
  • A request for a promotional card that contains only the app id, the operating system (iOS or Android), the cluster name "gaming" and whether the device is a phone or tablet. When a card is shown for about a second, a view ping is sent to the address in the card; opening a card sends a click request. These requests carry no advertising id, device id, account or location. Like any web request, the server we use sees the IP address of the connection.
  • Links you tap (our websites, this policy) open in your browser, which has its own privacy rules.

What we do not do

  • No advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites, no data sold or shared with third parties for their own use.
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

FPSAim is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can mute sound and haptics, delete all progress in Settings, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on a server.

Future ad support

A later version may show third-party ads. If that happens we will ask for your consent where the law requires it, offer non-personalised ads, and update this policy and the store privacy labels before release.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new date. Questions: support@playtools.top. Website: FPSAim support.

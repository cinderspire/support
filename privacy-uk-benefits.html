Privacy policy

Benefits Checker UK, published by Cinderspire. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any UK government department. Figures come from public information on GOV.UK and are estimates only.

In short

Benefits Checker UK has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. What you type stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (text size, colour mode), an unfinished check, and any households you choose to save. This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, is never uploaded, and is removed when you use Delete all my saved data in Settings or uninstall the app.

What the app sends over the internet

  • An optional request for a small suggestion card from the makers of this app. It contains the app identifier, the operating system name, a cluster name and whether the device is a phone or a tablet. It contains no name, no email, no advertising identifier and no location. When you tap a card, the link is opened in your browser and the click is counted on our server.
  • Links you choose to open (for example GOV.UK pages) open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data, we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share data.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It does not knowingly collect data from children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your local data is under your control in Settings.

Changes

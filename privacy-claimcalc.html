ClaimCalc privacy policy

Privacy policy of the ClaimCalc app by Cinderspire.

Last updated: 2026-11-09

The short version

ClaimCalc keeps everything you enter on your own device. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising identifier. Photos, documents, receipts, notes and every other record stay on your phone unless you choose to share a file or a PDF yourself.

What the app stores

Claim details, dates, costs, mileage, notes, a symptom journal, checklist progress and the photos and documents you attach are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Removing the app or using Settings, Erase all data, deletes them. Cinderspire has no copy and cannot recover them.

What leaves the device

  • Files you share. When you tap Create PDF and share, or share a photo, the system share sheet opens and you choose the destination. That transfer is between you and the app you pick.
  • Promotional cards. Where enabled, the app may request a small house promotional card from our own server. The request contains the app name, the platform (iOS), a content group and a device class (phone or tablet). It contains no identifiers, no claim data and no files, and nothing is used to track you across apps or sites. If the request fails, nothing is shown.
  • Links. Links to guides and to official sources open in your browser; that site's own policy then applies.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: only when you tap Take photo or Choose photo. Photos are copied into the app and not uploaded.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on date reminders. They are scheduled on the device.

Not legal advice

ClaimCalc is an organizer with educational worksheets and a general date reference. It is not a law firm, gives no legal advice and does not refer lawyers. Talk to a licensed attorney about your own situation.

Children

The app is meant for adults handling a claim and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published here. Questions: support@playtools.top (Cinderspire).

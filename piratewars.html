What Pirate Wars is

Command a sloop, frigate or galleon in fast naval battles. Play Team Battle (pirates against the Royal Navy), Four Fleets or Individual, with Sink the Fleet, Multi-Flag or Survival rules. Fight bots on your own, or host an online room for up to 8 captains and share its code with your friends.

This version shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. It needs no account, and solo battles need no internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do I play online with friends?

Open Online Battle and tap Host Online, then Create Room. The lobby shows a room code: share it with your friends (tap the code to copy it). They open Online Battle, tap Join Online and enter the code. Up to 8 captains can join a room, and the host chooses the mode and starts the battle.

There is no game server: the host's device runs the match and the other devices connect to it directly.

My friend cannot join my room
  1. Check that the code was typed exactly as shown.
  2. The host must keep the game open on the lobby screen until everyone has joined.
  3. Some networks block peer-to-peer play (some office, school or hotel Wi-Fi and some mobile carriers). Try another Wi-Fi network or mobile data on either device.

If it still fails, email us with both devices' models and the networks you tried.

What happens if the host leaves?

The host's device runs the match, so the battle ends for everyone when the host leaves or loses connection. If your own connection drops, the game shows Connection lost and takes you back to the menu.

Is there chat?

No. There is no chat of any kind, so players cannot send messages to each other. Captain names pass a word filter, any player can be muted in the lobby or the scoreboard, the host can remove a guest from the room, and Report a player in Settings emails us. A room can only be joined by people who have its code.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. This version of Pirate Wars shows no ads and offers nothing to buy. Every ship, mode and rule set is available to every player.

How do I change the camera?

In the settings, choose Camera: Close, Normal or Far. During a battle you can drag to look around, and on a touch screen pinch to zoom in or out.

Where is my data stored?

Your captain name, settings and battle statistics are stored on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the game deletes them with it. Online play sends your captain name and match data only to the players in your room. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The game crashes, or a feature does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Fully close the game and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your device.
  3. Lower the graphics quality in the settings on older devices.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your device model, iOS or Android version, the game version (shown in the settings) and what you tapped just before it went wrong.

What Gaugeful does

Gaugeful is a running-cost diary for drivers in the UK and Germany. Keep a garage of several vehicles (petrol, diesel, hybrid, electric), log every fill-up or charge, and see what each mile or kilometre costs. Everything stays on your iPhone or iPad: no account, no server.

  • Garage. Several vehicles, each with its own log, units and tank or battery size. Switch vehicle from the header of every screen.
  • Fill-ups and charges. Real consumption from full tank to full tank; EV charging as home or public with your own home tariff; other costs such as service, MOT, tax, insurance, tyres, parking and tolls.
  • Insights. Monthly spend, cost per mile or kilometre, consumption trend with a rolling average, home versus public charging, and an EV-versus-petrol comparison.
  • Reminders. MOT / HU-TÜV, road tax, insurance and service by date or odometer reading, with optional local alerts.
  • Journeys. Multi-stop trip costing with tolls, parking and congestion charges, shareable as a card.
  • Backup. JSON backup and restore, CSV export and import.
  • Converter. mpg (UK and US), L/100 km, km/L, miles per kWh and kWh per 100 km.

You type every price yourself, so the app needs no internet connection. English and German, with UK and metric units.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do I restore a purchase?

Pro is tied to the Apple Account that paid for it, so you can bring it back on another device, or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the device with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Gaugeful, go to the Garage tab and tap Garage Pro.
  3. Tap Restore purchase.

If the app says no purchase was found, check that you are online and signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. If it still does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it.

What does Pro add?

Everything for day-to-day use is free: a garage of two vehicles, unlimited fill-ups, charges and costs, insights, reminders, journeys, backup and restore, and the converter. Pro is a one-time purchase that lifts the vehicle limit, adds CSV export and keeps unlimited saved journeys. The app has no ads, with or without Pro.

How is real consumption worked out?

From full tank to full tank. Add each fill-up with the odometer reading and the amount of fuel; when you next fill up completely, the app divides the fuel used since the last full tank by the distance driven. Top-ups in between (switch off “Filled to full”) count towards the next full tank. For an electric vehicle the same idea works with kWh and charges marked “Charged to full”, with odometer readings.

Where do the fuel and electricity prices come from?

From you. The app never downloads prices — you type what you paid, so figures match your own receipts. For home charging, set your tariff once in the vehicle's settings.

How do reminders and alerts work?

Open Garage → Reminders, add MOT / inspection, road tax, insurance or service, and choose a date, an odometer reading, or both. Alerts are optional: only when you switch a reminder's alert on does your device ask whether Gaugeful may send notifications. Alerts are scheduled on the device itself. If you declined, enable notifications for Gaugeful in the Settings app.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Garage → Backup and restore. Save a backup creates a JSON file, kept in the Files app (On My iPhone → Gaugeful) and offered through the share sheet. To restore on another device, copy the file there and choose it under Restore; it replaces what is on the device, after a safety copy is written. CSV export (Pro) and CSV import work with spreadsheets.

Does Gaugeful work offline?

Yes. Everything works without an internet connection. You only need to be online to buy or restore Pro, or to open this page or the Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language or units?

Language and appearance are under Garage → Settings. Units and currency belong to each vehicle: open a vehicle in the Garage tab to change miles/kilometres, mpg/L per 100 km, litres/gallons, pounds/euros and price units.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, find the purchase and choose Request a refund. If something is broken, please email us as well; we would much rather fix it.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, inside the app. There is no account to create, and data does not sync between devices. Gaugeful collects no data: nothing you enter is sent to us, and the app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. Deleting the app deletes its data (back up first if you want to keep it). More in the Privacy Policy.

The app crashes, or a feature does not work. What should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart the device.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your device model, iOS version, the app version (Garage → Settings), the screen you were on and what you tapped.

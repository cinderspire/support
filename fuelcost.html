What Fuel Cost does

Fuel Cost works out what a journey costs in fuel or electricity: one way or return, split between passengers, and for regular driving such as a weekly commute. Its fuel log records your fill-ups and works out your real consumption from full tank to full tank. A converter switches between MPG, L/100 km, km/L, miles/kWh and kWh/100 km.

You type every price yourself, so the app needs no internet connection. It is available in English and German, with UK and metric units.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do I restore a purchase?

Pro is tied to the Apple Account that paid for it, so you can bring it back on another iPhone, or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the iPhone with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Fuel Cost and tap the Settings tab.
  3. Under Purchases, tap Restore Purchases.

The same Restore Purchases button is also on the Pro screen. If the app says no purchase was found, check that you are online and signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. If it still does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

What does Pro add?

Everything in the calculators is free. The fuel log is free too and keeps every fill-up you enter; the free version uses the last 10 fill-ups for its figures (average consumption, cost per distance, totals).

Pro is a one-time purchase that uses every fill-up in the figures and adds CSV export of your fuel log, for a spreadsheet. The iPhone app has no ads, with or without Pro.

How is real consumption worked out?

From full tank to full tank. Fill the tank completely, note the odometer, and add each fill-up with the litres (or gallons), the price and the odometer reading. When you next fill up completely, the app divides the fuel used since the last full tank by the distance driven. Partial fill-ups are counted towards the next full tank, so the result stays correct.

Where do the fuel prices come from?

From you. The app does not download live prices — you type the price you pay, so the result matches your own pump. Units can be changed on each screen (miles or kilometres, MPG or L/100 km, pence or euros per litre, kWh for electric cars) and in Settings.

Does Fuel Cost work offline?

Yes. All calculations and the fuel log work without an internet connection. You only need to be online to buy or restore Pro, or to open this page or the Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language or units?

Open the Settings tab. Under Language choose Phone, English or Deutsch. Under Units pick currency (£ or €), distance, fuel consumption, fuel amount, fuel price and electricity units. Phone follows your iPhone: German for a German iPhone, English otherwise.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Fuel Cost does not have any subscriptions — Pro is a one-time payment, so there is nothing recurring to cancel. If you are looking for a subscription from one of our other apps, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or go to Manage your Apple subscriptions.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and we cannot issue or reverse a payment — only Apple can do that.

  • Apple (App Store): go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If you are asking for a refund because something is broken, please email us as well. We would much rather fix the problem.

Where is my data stored?

Your trips, fuel log and settings are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account to create and no password to remember, and data does not sync between devices.

Fuel Cost collects no data: nothing you enter is sent to us, and the app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. Deleting the app deletes its data. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.

The app crashes, or a feature does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version of Fuel Cost in the App Store — the fix may already be out.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version (Settings, under About), the screen you were on and what you tapped — and a screenshot if you have one.

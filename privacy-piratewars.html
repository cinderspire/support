Privacy Policy

P

Pirate Wars: Ship Battle

Ship-to-ship naval battles against bots, or online with friends over a direct peer-to-peer connection. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

Battles against bots work offline and send nothing anywhere. Your captain name, settings and Captain Log statistics are saved on your device. The game shows no ads, offers no purchases, has no account and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

If you choose Online Battle, your device connects directly to the devices of the players in your room. We run no game server, so nothing about you or your matches reaches us.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number to play.

What the game handles

Nothing is sent to us. Here is everything the game handles, and where it goes.

  • Captain name. You type it in the settings, or the game picks a random pirate name. It is stored on your device. In an online match it is shown to the other players in your room. Names pass a word filter, and there is no free-text chat.
  • Settings and Captain Log. Language, sound, graphics quality, camera distance and your battle statistics are stored on your device only.
  • Online match data. When you host or join a room, your device exchanges game data with the other devices in that room: your captain name, ship class, team and colour, your ship's movement and shots, and the quick-chat phrases you pick from a fixed list. This data travels between the devices in the room, and through a relay only when a direct connection is blocked (see below). It is not stored on any server of ours.
  • Room codes. A room can only be joined with its code. There is no public lobby or matchmaking, so you play with the people you share the code with.

Third-party services (online play only)

These free public services are used only when you host or join an online room. Solo battles use none of them.

  • PeerJS cloud server (run by the open-source PeerJS project, peerjs.com) helps the devices in a room find each other. It receives a random room or player code and the technical connection details your device offers, which include your IP address. Game data does not pass through it.
  • Google public STUN server (stun.l.google.com) tells your device its public network address so that a direct connection can be made. It sees your IP address.
  • PeerJS relay (TURN) servers are used only when your network blocks a direct connection. The encrypted game data is then passed through them.
  • As with every peer-to-peer connection, the devices you connect to can see your IP address. The connection between devices is encrypted (WebRTC with DTLS).
  • We do not control these services; their own privacy policies apply.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the game shows no ads
  • your real name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • location
  • photos, camera or microphone — the game uses none of them
  • payment card details
  • purchase information — this version offers no in-app purchases
  • analytics or usage statistics — the game includes no analytics SDK
  • crash reports — the game includes no crash-reporting SDK

There is no account, no sign-up and no login, and no developer-run server. We have nothing about you to look at, hand over or sell.

Purchases

This version offers no in-app purchases, so the game handles no payment or purchase information at all.

Your choices and rights

Your data is on your device, so you are the one holding it.

  • Play solo against bots and the game makes no internet connection at all.
  • Change your captain name at any time in the settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device.

Asking us anything. Email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request, including a request to access or delete data. We hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, so in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Pirate Wars: Ship Battle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, such as a new online feature or a service we stop using, we update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

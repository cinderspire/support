Privacy Policy

F

Fuel Cost: Trip Calculator

Trip costs for petrol, diesel or electric cars, and a fuel log that works out your real consumption. English and German. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version

Fuel Cost collects no data. Everything you type — distances, consumption, prices, your fill-ups and your settings — is stored on your iPhone and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing you enter. Trip details, prices, units, your fuel log and your settings are stored on your iPhone. All calculations run on the phone and work offline. The app does not download prices or anything else from the internet, and there is no cloud sync.
  • Whether you own Pro. Apple's App Store tells the app whether you own the Pro purchase, and the app keeps that answer on your iPhone.
  • Only what you choose to share. CSV export (Pro) creates a file on your iPhone and opens the iOS share sheet; the file goes only where you send it. If you write to support@playtools.top, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • location — the app never uses GPS; you type distances yourself
  • photos
  • browsing history
  • payment card details
  • usage analytics — the app includes no analytics SDK
  • crash reports — the app includes no crash-reporting SDK
  • advertising identifiers — the iPhone app shows no ads and includes no ad SDK
  • identifiers that could track you across other companies' apps or websites — the app does no tracking

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything the app saves is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

The Pro purchase is processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details, and the app keeps only one thing from the App Store: whether you own Pro.

Third-party services

Apple's App Store is the only outside service the app talks to. It has its own privacy policy.

  • Apple's App Store — Shows the Pro price, processes the purchase, restores it, and tells the app whether you own it. Apple privacy policy.

The Privacy Policy link and the support email in the app open only when you tap them. Like any website, the site that serves a page receives the ordinary information a browser sends to load it.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your iPhone, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • In the Fuel log, swipe a fill-up to the left to delete it (you can undo for a few seconds).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. Your Pro purchase is kept, because it belongs to your Apple Account — use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Fuel Cost is made for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 — there is no account or sign-up, and nothing about the user is sent to us. If you have a question about a child's use of the app, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a change in the services it uses — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

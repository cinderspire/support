Privacy Policy

G

Gaugeful: Fuel & Trip Costs

A running-cost diary: garage, fill-ups, EV charging, insights, reminders, journeys and backup. English and German. Formerly published as Fuel Cost. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Gaugeful collects no data. Everything you enter — vehicles, fill-ups, charges, costs, reminders, journeys and settings — is stored on your device and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and there is no developer-run server holding your content.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Concretely, here is everything that is handled or sent anywhere.

  • Nothing you enter. Your garage and everything logged for it, your reminders, saved journeys and settings are stored on your device. All calculations run on the device and work offline. The app does not download prices or anything else from the internet, and there is no cloud sync.
  • Reminders and notifications. If you switch a reminder's alert on, the device asks for permission to show notifications. Alerts are scheduled locally on the device by the operating system; no notification service or server of ours is involved, and nothing leaves the device.
  • Files you choose. Backups (JSON) and CSV exports are created on your device, saved in the app's folder in the Files app and offered through the iOS share sheet; they go only where you send them. Restoring or importing opens the system file picker and reads only the file you choose. Shared journey cards are images created on the device and shared the same way.
  • Whether you own Pro. Apple's App Store tells the app whether you own the Pro purchase, and the app keeps that answer on the device.
  • Only what you choose to send us. If you write to support@playtools.top, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply to you.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • your name
  • your email address or phone number
  • contacts
  • location — the app never uses GPS; you type distances yourself
  • photos
  • browsing history
  • payment card details
  • usage analytics — the app includes no analytics SDK
  • crash reports — the app includes no crash-reporting SDK
  • advertising identifiers — the iOS app shows no ads and includes no ad SDK
  • identifiers that could track you across other companies' apps or websites — the app does no tracking

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything the app saves is stored on your device, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

The Pro purchase is processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details, and the app keeps only one thing from the App Store: whether you own Pro.

Third-party services

Apple's App Store is the only outside service the app talks to. It has its own privacy policy.

  • Apple's App Store — Shows the Pro price, processes the purchase, restores it, and tells the app whether you own it. Apple privacy policy.

The Privacy Policy link and the support email in the app open only when you tap them. Like any website, the site that serves a page receives the ordinary information a browser sends to load it.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • Swipe an entry to the left in the Log to delete it (you can undo for a few seconds); delete a vehicle in the Garage tab to remove it with everything logged for it; Garage → Backup and restore → Erase everything clears the whole garage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device, but not files you saved in the Files app. Your Pro purchase is kept, because it belongs to your Apple Account — use Restore purchase after reinstalling.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Gaugeful is made for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 — there is no account or sign-up, and nothing about the user is sent to us. If you have a question about a child's use of the app, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a change in the services it uses — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

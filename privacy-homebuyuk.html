Homebuy UK privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · published by Cinderspire

Homebuy UK is a home buying calculator for the UK. It works on your device and does not need an account.

What stays on your device

Your price, deposit, rate, saved scenarios, checklist ticks and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. We cannot see them and they are not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. When you share a PDF or text summary, you choose where it goes through your device's share sheet.

The one network request

On start the app reads a small settings file published on GitHub Pages (cinderspire.github.io) that says whether promotional cards are switched on; GitHub sees the request like any web request. If they are on, the app may ask our server for a small promotional card (for example for our own website or other apps). The request contains the app name, the platform (iOS or Android), a device class (phone or tablet) and the card group. It contains no advertising identifier, no account, no location and none of your figures. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the server, which we do not use to identify you. When you tap a card, the tap is counted by our server and the link opens in your browser or store.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK in this version.
  • No tracking across apps or websites and no sale of data.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Links

The app links to official sources (gov.uk, revenue.scot, gov.wales) and to ukcalculator.com. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is for adults buying property and is not directed at children.

Independence and advice

Homebuy UK is not affiliated with any government body or lender. It gives estimates only, not financial, tax or legal advice.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@playtools.top.

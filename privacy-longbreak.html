Longbreak: UK Leave Planner

Leave, bank holidays, rota and shift diary for UK workers. This policy covers this app only; each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Everything you enter in Longbreak (your leave, shifts, rota, allowance and settings) stays on your iPhone. The app collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content. Everything, including the bundled bank holiday dates, works offline.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything the app keeps or hands on.

  • What you enter stays on the device. Your region, work days, holiday allowance, booked leave (with any notes you type), rota, logged shifts, reminder choices and theme are stored inside the app on your iPhone. There is no cloud sync, so we cannot see them.
  • Reminders. If you switch reminders on, Longbreak asks iOS for permission to send notifications and schedules them locally on the phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time Pro purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • Files you choose to create. A calendar (.ics) file or a backup file is written to temporary storage and handed to the iOS share sheet only when you ask. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app you pick.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data (the iPhone app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK)
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • contacts, photos or files
  • location
  • your leave, shifts, rota or settings
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Longbreak Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details; Apple only tells the app whether Pro is unlocked.

Third-party services

The iPhone app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

  • Apple App Store handles the optional Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. Apple privacy policy.
  • Apple notifications deliver the local reminders you switch on. They are generated on the phone.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • Remove a booking or shift from its day, swipe a booking away in Leave & balance, or use Erase all data in Calendar & backup to clear everything.
  • Switch reminders off at any time, in the app or in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request, including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Longbreak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, such as a new feature that sends something online, we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.

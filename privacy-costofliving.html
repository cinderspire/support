Cost of Living UK

Privacy policy

Cost of Living UK, published by Cinderspire. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: there is no account and no tracking. What you enter and save stays on your device. The app asks our static data file for newer figures, and may show a small card about our own websites.

What stays on your device

Your amounts, chosen months, spending mix, places, salary, saved scenarios, theme choice and a copy of the latest downloaded data are stored on your phone only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app sends

  • Data updates. At most once every six hours the app requests two small public data files (prices and places). Like any web request, the server that hosts them sees the request, including the IP address, in its ordinary logs. The app adds no identifier, account or personal data.
  • Our own-website cards. After you have used the app, a slot may show a card about calculators on our own websites. To choose a card the app can ask our promotion service for one, sending only the app name, the platform (iOS or Android), a group name, and whether the device is a phone or tablet. No advertising identifier and no personal data are sent. Opening a card goes through our link service so we can count taps.
  • Links. Links to official sources and to our websites open in your browser. Their privacy policies apply there.

What the app does not do

No account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no location, contacts, photos or microphone, and no sharing of data with data brokers. If you press Share, the result image and text go only where you choose.

Data sources

Figures come from official open data published under the Open Government Licence v3.0 and are bundled with the app. See "About and sources" in the app.

Children

The app is for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. You can clear everything by deleting the app. If you have a question, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear at the top.

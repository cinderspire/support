What Sea of Shoguns is

Lead one of four rival sea clans in a fictional archipelago torn apart by war. Each season you collect koku from your provinces, recruit swift kobaya, sturdy sekibune and towering atakebune, and send your fleets along the sea lanes. Typhoons, winter seas and good harvests change your plans from turn to turn.

When fleets meet, you take command in a real-time 3D battle: steer your ships for the broadside, set enemy decks ablaze with fire arrows, ram, board and capture enemy ships, and rally your crews with war drums. Wind, fog and storms shape every fight. Pause whenever you need to think.

Prefer a single fight? Skirmish lets you build a fleet on a points budget, pick the location and weather, and battle the AI admiral right away. Six challenges are rated with up to three stars, a short tutorial teaches the controls, and achievements and battle statistics track your progress. The game is single-player, works offline, shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases.

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How does the campaign work?

The campaign is turn-based, and every turn is one season. Your provinces pay income in koku. Spend it to recruit kobaya, sekibune and atakebune at your ports, move your fleets along the sea lanes and attack rival clans. Win the sea battle at a province and it becomes yours. Hold most of the archipelago, or take every rival capital, to win. Lose your own capital and the campaign is over.

How do I control my ships in a battle?

Tap one of your ships to select it, tap the sea to move, and tap an enemy ship to attack. Your ships fire on their own when an enemy is in range, so your job is the manoeuvre. Use the ability buttons for ramming speed, fire arrows, boarding, war drums and chain shot. Hold and drag to select several ships. Drag to move the camera, pinch to zoom and twist two fingers to rotate it. Pause the battle at any time: you can still give orders while time is stopped.

Do I have to fight every battle myself?

No. When fleets meet in the campaign you can command the battle yourself or let it be resolved automatically. The result carries over: damaged ships stay damaged until they repair in a friendly port, and captured ships join the winner's fleet.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The campaign saves automatically after every turn and every battle, and Continue on the main menu takes you back. Saves are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them, and there is no cloud save.

Does the game need an internet connection, ads or purchases?

No. Sea of Shoguns is a single-player game that works fully offline. This version shows no ads, offers no in-app purchases and needs no account.

Which languages does the game support?

English, Japanese, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean, Simplified Chinese and Russian. The game follows your device language, and you can change it in the settings.

The game runs slowly or something is broken
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Lower the graphics quality in the settings on older devices.
  3. Fully close the game and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your device.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your device model, iOS version, the game version (shown in the settings) and what you were doing when it went wrong.

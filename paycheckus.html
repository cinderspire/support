FAQ

Does Paycheck Planner need an account?

No. It works offline and keeps everything on your phone.

Which tax year and states are covered?

Tax year 2026, all 50 states and Washington, DC. Each state shows its sources and the date its figures were checked; a state is marked provisional on the result screen while its official 2026 figures are incomplete.

How is federal tax calculated?

With the percentage method that payroll software uses (IRS Publication 15-T for 2026), including your W-4 settings, then Social Security, Medicare and Additional Medicare.

Are city and county taxes included?

No. You can enter your own local tax rate under Pre-tax and other deductions.

Is this tax advice?

No. Results are estimates. Paycheck Planner is independent and is not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency.

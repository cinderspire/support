What the app does

Degree & Grade Calculator UK (shown as “UK Grades” on your home screen) works offline and without an account:

  • Degree class from module credits and marks, with the year weighting your university uses, “What you need” for unfinished modules and a borderline checklist.
  • Mark to grade: GCSE 9–1, Combined Science and A level grades from the boundaries you enter or a dated example table.
  • Tariff points for 2026 and 2027 entry, with a target.
  • Saved scenarios: 3 free; unlimited plus CSV export with UK Grades Pro.

How do I restore UK Grades Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open UK Grades and go to Settings (or the UK Grades Pro screen).
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

If it still does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

Is UK Grades Pro a subscription?

No. UK Grades Pro is a one-time purchase charged to your Apple ID. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. It unlocks unlimited saved scenarios (the free version keeps 3) and CSV export of your saved scenarios. Every calculator is free.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would much rather fix it.

How is my degree class worked out?

Each year’s average is weighted by module credits, then the years are combined with the weighting you pick (for example 30/70 or 40/60, or your own). The result is placed on the usual scale: First 70+, 2:1 60–69, 2:2 50–59, Third 40–49 (Distinction, Merit, Pass for a Masters). Universities have their own rules — rounding, borderline policies, dropped modules — so check your course handbook. The app’s result is an estimate; your university decides.

Where do the grade boundaries come from?

Boundaries change every year and differ by exam board, subject and tier, so you enter the boundaries for your own paper. The app also includes a dated example table (June 2025 series) you can start from and edit. Always use your exam board’s published boundaries for real decisions.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version and what you tapped just before it went wrong — a screenshot helps.

What the app does

Quillmark keeps your studies in one journal. It works offline, needs no account and runs on iPhone and iPad:

  • Degree: modules by year with credits, a final mark or several weighted assessments, your class on a seal-and-gauge, the average you need in everything not yet marked for the class you are aiming at, a “try a score” slider and a borderline checklist.
  • Planner: coursework and exam deadlines linked to modules, with optional reminders (local notifications) a week, three days or a day before, or on the day.
  • Exams: GCSE, Combined Science, AS and A level subjects with a target and predicted grade, a mock log with a trend line, grades from the boundaries you enter or a dated example table, and a grade finder.
  • Offers: type offers such as AAB, A*AA, ABB+, 128 points or DDM and compare them with your predicted grades and tariff points (2026 and 2027 entry).
  • Share and keep: a result card, a backup file or text block, and with Pro a PDF study record and a CSV.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Quillmark Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling.

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open Quillmark, tap the sliders icon at the top right (the study desk) and open Quillmark Pro.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

If it still does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it.

Is Quillmark Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase charged to your Apple ID. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. It adds the PDF study record, CSV export, up to six plans, extra result card styles and unlimited saved checks. Everything else in the app is free.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would much rather fix it.

How is my degree class worked out?

Each year’s average is weighted by module credits (a module with several assessments is the weighted sum of them), then the years are combined with the weighting you pick, for example 30/70 or 40/60, or your own. The result is placed on the usual scale: First 70+, 2:1 60–69, 2:2 50–59, Third 40–49 (Distinction, Merit, Pass for a Masters). Universities have their own rules for rounding, borderline cases and dropped modules, so check your programme handbook. The result is an estimate; your university decides.

How does “what you need” work?

Everything that is not marked yet is treated as one block. Quillmark works out the single average mark that block would need to reach the boundary of the class you chose, using your credits and weights. Because the final average moves in a straight line with the unknown marks, the answer is exact for the numbers you entered. It does not predict your marks.

How do reminders work, and what does the notification permission do?

Reminders are local notifications created by your own phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. Your phone asks for permission only at the moment you switch your first reminder on; if you say no, the app keeps working and explains how to allow notifications in Settings later. You can switch reminders off, or turn notifications off for the app in iOS Settings, at any time.

Where do the grade boundaries come from?

Boundaries change every year and differ by exam board, subject and tier, so you enter the boundaries for your own paper. The app also includes a dated example table (Pearson Edexcel, June 2025 series, six subjects) you can start from and edit. The grade shown for a mock is only as accurate as the boundaries you enter. Use your exam board’s published boundaries for real decisions.

How does the offers check read what I type?

It reads grade strings (AAB, A*AA, ABB+, AAB or ABB), BTEC grades (DDM) and points (128 points). Letters are compared one place at a time, best grade against best grade, with your predicted A levels; points are compared with your tariff total. A plus after the grades is kept as a reminder that the university added a condition. Anything it cannot read is shown as it was typed, for you to check by hand. A university can change or withdraw an offer, and only it decides whether you have met it.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open the study desk (sliders icon), choose Copy backup text or Share a backup file, and keep it somewhere safe, for example in Notes or an e-mail to yourself. On the new phone open Restore from a backup, paste the text, and confirm. Restoring replaces what is on the phone.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your device.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your device model, iOS version and what you tapped just before it went wrong — a screenshot helps.

What the app does

Stone Weight Tracker UK is an offline weight diary for the UK: log weigh-ins in stones and pounds, pounds or kilograms, see a trend chart, week and month changes and goal progress, convert between st, lb and kg, and look at BMI (with the NHS adult bands) and a maintenance-calorie estimate. Figures are general information only, not medical advice.

The app is free, with no ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks charts beyond 30 days, several goals, CSV export and colour themes.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app, tap Settings (gear icon), then Restore Purchases — or open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases there.
  3. You will see “Pro restored.” when it is back.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would rather fix it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone, so the app works offline. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

How are stones and pounds converted?

The app uses 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg and 14 lb = 1 stone, and shows pounds to 0.1 (or the nearest half pound if you choose). Entries are stored exactly, so switching units never loses accuracy.

Is this medical advice?

The app is an independent tracker and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHS or any government body. BMI and calorie figures are general information, not medical advice: for questions about your weight or health, talk to your GP or another registered professional.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. Your weigh-ins, goals and settings never leave the phone. There is no account, and the iPhone app collects no data. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the inputs you used and what you expected — a screenshot helps.

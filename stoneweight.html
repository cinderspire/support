What the app does

Stonewise is a weight diary for people who think in stones and pounds. Record weigh-ins in stones and pounds, pounds or kilograms; read your latest weight on a scale dial; see a trend, your weekly pace and, once you set a target, a goal date with a window either side. Track waist, hips, chest and other measurements, keep progress photos, get one reminder a week, earn milestones, and back up or import your data. It also includes a converter, BMI with the NHS adult bands and a maintenance-calorie estimate. All figures are general information only, not medical advice.

Everything is stored on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and no ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds chart ranges beyond 30 days, more than one target, unlimited progress photos, CSV export and accent colours.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can bring it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app, go to Toolkit then Settings and tap Restore Purchases — or open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases there.
  3. You will see “Welcome back: Pro is restored on this device.” when it is back.

If it does not come back, email us and tell us roughly when you bought it — we will help you sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details and cannot issue or reverse a payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

If something is broken, please email us as well — we would rather fix it.

How do progress photos work?

Open Body, then Photos, and tap Choose a photo. iOS shows its own photo picker, so Stonewise only receives the pictures you choose and never asks for access to your whole library. Each picture is copied into the app’s private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is shared unless you share it yourself. Deleting a photo in Stonewise does not touch your photo library.

How does the weekly reminder work?

Turn on Weekly check-in in the Toolkit or in Settings and choose a day and time. It is a local notification on your device and needs no internet connection. Stonewise asks iOS for notification permission only at the moment you switch the reminder on. If you said no, allow notifications for Stonewise in the iOS Settings app and switch the reminder on again.

How do I back up, move or import my data?
  1. Open Toolkit, then Backup and import.
  2. Tap Share backup file and save the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, email to yourself), or tap Copy backup text.
  3. On the new device choose Choose a file (or Paste text instead), then pick Merge or Replace.

The same screen reads a CSV from a spreadsheet: a date column and a weight column, with weights such as 82.5, 182 lb or 13 st 2 lb. Progress photos stay in the app’s own storage and are not inside the backup file; only their dates and notes are.

What does the goal date mean?

Stonewise fits a straight line through your last four weeks of weigh-ins and extends it to your target. The window around the date shows how uncertain that line is: noisy weigh-ins give a wide window, steady ones a narrow one. It describes the trend in your own numbers. It is not a promise, a prediction about your health, or advice.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device, so the app works offline. The App Store needs a connection only when you buy or restore Pro.

How are stones and pounds converted?

The app uses 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg and 14 lb = 1 stone, and shows pounds to 0.1 (or the nearest half pound if you choose). Entries are stored exactly, so switching units never loses accuracy.

Is this medical advice?

The app is an independent tracker and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHS or any government body. BMI and calorie figures are general information, not medical advice: for questions about your weight or health, talk to your GP or another registered professional.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. Your weigh-ins, measurements, targets, settings and progress photos never leave the device. There is no account, and the app collects no data. Progress photos are copied into the app’s private storage when you pick them. Deleting the app, or using Settings, Erase everything, deletes everything it stored. Details: Privacy Policy.

Something does not work — what should I do?
  1. Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that does not help, restart your iPhone.

Still broken? Email us at support@playtools.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, the inputs you used and what you expected — a screenshot helps.

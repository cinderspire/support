Privacy Policy

S

Stonewise

A private weight diary in stones, pounds and kilograms, with measurements and progress photos. This policy covers this app only — each of our apps has its own.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Everything you enter or choose — weigh-ins, notes, targets, body measurements, progress photos, height, age and settings — stays on your iPhone or iPad. The iPhone app collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no developer-run server holding your content. The app works fully offline and has no connection to Apple Health.

What the app handles

Concretely, here is everything the app keeps or hands on.

  • What you type stays on the device. Weigh-ins (date, weight, optional note), targets, body measurements, height, age, activity level, unit, reminder and look choices are stored inside the app. There is no cloud sync and no HealthKit access, so we cannot see them.
  • Progress photos you choose. When you pick a photo, iOS shows its own photo picker and the app receives only the pictures you select. They are copied into the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not ask for access to your photo library, does not use the camera, and never uploads or shares a photo. Removing a photo in the app deletes its copy; your photo library is not touched.
  • A weekly reminder, if you switch it on. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. The app asks iOS for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on. No notification content or token is sent anywhere.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked. Apple tells the app whether you have bought or restored the one-time Pro purchase; the app remembers that on the device.
  • Backups, CSV files and sharing. When you export a backup or CSV, or share a progress note, the file goes where you send it through the iOS share sheet. Importing reads a file you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following.

  • advertising data — the iPhone app shows no ads and contains no ad SDK
  • analytics, usage or crash data
  • your name, email address or phone number
  • your contacts, your photo library or any file you did not choose yourself
  • photos you add to the diary (they stay on your device)
  • location
  • your weight, measurements, height, age, targets or any health information
  • payment card details

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. Everything you create in the app is stored on your iPhone, so we have no way to look at it, and nothing to hand over or sell.

Payments

Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) processed by Apple on the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details — Apple only tells the app whether Pro is unlocked.

Third-party services

The iPhone app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

  • Apple App Store — handles the optional Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. Apple privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your device, you are the one holding it.

Deleting your data.

  • You can delete any weigh-in, measurement or photo in the app, or use “Erase everything” in Settings to remove all weigh-ins, measurements, photos and the reminder at once.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Asking us anything. You may email support@playtools.top with any privacy question or request — including a request to access or delete data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server of ours, in most cases the honest answer is that there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase, and we will tell you so plainly.

Children

Stonewise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email support@playtools.top and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new feature that sends something online, or a service we stop using — we update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.
